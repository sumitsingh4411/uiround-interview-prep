# Contributing

Thanks for helping make these questions better. Most help comes in through issues, and each form asks for what is needed:

- [Report a mistake in a question](https://github.com/sumitsingh4411/uiround-interview-prep/issues/new?template=content-error.yml): a wrong answer, a failing test, a typo or an unclear problem.
- [Request a question or topic](https://github.com/sumitsingh4411/uiround-interview-prep/issues/new?template=question-request.yml): a question you were asked in an interview, or a topic that should be covered.
- [Report a bug on the site](https://github.com/sumitsingh4411/uiround-interview-prep/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml): something on [uiround.com](https://uiround.com) is broken or looks wrong.
- [Request a feature](https://github.com/sumitsingh4411/uiround-interview-prep/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml): something you want the site to do.

Pull requests that fix a typo or a broken link in this repository are welcome.

Please keep payment, account and privacy problems out of public issues: email [sumitsinghfrontend@gmail.com](mailto:sumitsinghfrontend@gmail.com) instead. To report a security problem, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
