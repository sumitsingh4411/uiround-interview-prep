# FAANG frontend interview preparation: questions and a plan

How to prepare for a frontend engineer interview at Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Microsoft and other big-tech companies, at SDE-1, SDE-2 or senior level. What each round tests, the company guides, and free FAANG-level frontend interview questions you can solve with tests on [UIRound](https://uiround.com).

## How big-tech frontend loops differ

Big-tech frontend loops test the same skills as other product companies, with a higher bar and more rounds:

- **General coding.** Several big-tech companies give frontend candidates the same data structures and algorithms rounds as other engineers. Others ask JavaScript and UI coding instead. Read the company guide before you plan.
- **UI built from scratch,** often in plain JavaScript without a framework: a component, a widget or a small app, with accessibility and edge cases.
- **Frontend system design** from SDE-2 upwards, and at senior levels often the round that decides the level you are offered.
- **Behavioral rounds with a framework.** Some companies score these against published leadership principles or values; prepare stories for each.

Each guide below lists the rounds and what each tests, from public sources: [Google](https://uiround.com/companies/google) · [Meta](https://uiround.com/companies/meta) · [Amazon](https://uiround.com/companies/amazon) · [Microsoft](https://uiround.com/companies/microsoft) · [Apple](https://uiround.com/companies/apple) · [Netflix](https://uiround.com/companies/netflix) · [all companies](https://uiround.com/companies).

## An eight-week plan

| Weeks | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 2 | DSA patterns in JavaScript: arrays, hash maps, two pointers, trees, graphs |
| 3 to 4 | JavaScript utilities and UI built from scratch, with accessibility |
| 5 to 6 | Frontend system design: the course, then two timed designs a week |
| 7 | Behavioral stories mapped to the company's values |
| 8 | Mock interviews and review of every mistake |

For the longer road, from college to a big-tech frontend offer, read the [tier-3 college to FAANG roadmap](https://uiround.com/faang-roadmap).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Do FAANG frontend interviews ask LeetCode-style DSA?</b></summary>

Several big-tech companies do, in the same coding rounds as other software engineers, usually at medium difficulty. Others focus on JavaScript and UI coding. Check the [company guide](https://uiround.com/companies) for the loop you are in, and see [how much DSA frontend interviews need](https://uiround.com/guides/dsa-for-frontend-interviews).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I crack a FAANG frontend interview from a tier-3 college?</b></summary>

Yes. Big-tech interviews score what you do in the room, not your college. The harder part is getting the interview: referrals, a strong resume and often a first job at a product company. The [FAANG roadmap](https://uiround.com/faang-roadmap) lays out the route.

</details>
